# Azure architecture icons — source & terms

`azure-ai-foundry.svg` and `azure-openai.svg` are official Microsoft **Azure
architecture icons**, downloaded from:
https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/icons/

Microsoft permits these icons in **architectural diagrams, training materials, or
documentation**. In AI Arena they are used **only** inside the "How it works"
architecture diagram (`src/components/ArchitectureDiagram.tsx`), with the product
name shown next to each icon — a permitted use.

They are used **as-is** (not cropped, flipped, rotated, or distorted) and are
**not** used to brand or represent AI Arena itself. Product/brand *logos* are not
used anywhere (those require a Microsoft license). See the T-017 ticket and
docs/adr for the compliance rationale.
